# Weekly Goal Sheet — Agent Instructions

## What this is

Your Weekly Goal Sheet is where you set your numbers for the week and track them daily. It's built around one idea: **consistent daily action — not perfection — is what builds a lasting business.**

There are two versions:
- **Online Weekly Goal Sheet** — use this whenever you have a laptop or desktop. It calculates your percentages automatically and remembers your goals week to week.
- **Paper Worksheet** — use this when you're away from a computer (in a meeting, at a coaching session, on the go). Fill it out by hand, then transfer the numbers into the online sheet as soon as you're back at a computer.

## Getting started

1. Open the Weekly Goal Sheet and enter your **name** and the **week** you're setting goals for.
2. Work through each section below, filling in your goals before the week begins.
3. Log your actual activity **daily** — don't wait until Friday. The steppers (+ / −) make it quick to update as you go.

## Lead Generation

- The team standard is **9:30 AM – 12:30 PM, with a 2-hour minimum** of focused lead generation every day.
- If you can hit that window, log your start and end time for the day.
- If a conflict comes up (an appointment, a doctor's visit, a client who's only free in the morning), that's fine — use **"+ Add time block"** to log a second time block later in the day (e.g. 3:00–4:00 PM). Your total time for the day is what counts toward the 2-hour goal.
- Each day shows a big status marker: a red **X** if you haven't hit 2 hours yet, or a **flame + "Great Job!"** once you have. It flashes when your status changes, then settles — that's your visual cue to check in on your progress.

## Core Success Metrics

This section is what matters most — these are the numbers that actually predict a closed transaction, not just busywork.

**Two-Way Conversations Goal** (combined)
- This is one shared goal fed by two activities: **Two-Way Conversations** (a real back-and-forth — a phone call, or even a conversation at Walmart where real estate comes up and the other person knows you're an agent) and **Two-Way Text Conversations** (an actual exchange — they replied, not just a text you sent).
- Set your **Monthly Goal** once — after you enter it and click away, it locks in for the month. This keeps you honest to the number you committed to.
- Set your **Weekly Goal** — how much of that monthly number you're committing to this week. It also locks once entered.
- **Important:** if you set a weekly goal below the pace your monthly goal needs, you'll see a caution message telling you exactly how much more your remaining weeks will need to average to still hit your month. Pay attention to it — it's not a scolding, it's math helping you plan.
- Log your actual conversations and texts as they happen throughout the week using the steppers.

**Leads Generated, Appointments Set, Appointments Held, Listings Taken, Buyer Agreements Signed, Contracts Written/Offers Submitted, Closings**
- Each of these has its own Monthly Goal and Weekly Goal — same locking behavior (enter once, it locks).
- These are separate from the conversations goal — they don't combine with each other.

## Supporting Activities

Calls Made, Emails Sent, Database Touches, FB/IG Engagement, Open Houses, Referrals Given/Received, Reviews Requested/Received — these are still worth tracking (especially FB/IG for brand awareness), but they're **not part of your weekly goal**. Just log your counts here for your own visibility.

## A few important things to know

- **Once a goal is locked, it can't be edited** — the only way to change it is hitting **"Start Fresh Week,"** which resets your counts for a new week (your goals carry over from last week so you don't have to retype them, but they unlock so you can adjust).
- Your data is saved automatically in **your browser, on your device** — it doesn't sync to other devices or to a central database. Use the same computer/browser each week so your history and monthly totals stay accurate.
- If you started your week on the paper worksheet, transfer those numbers into the online sheet as soon as you can — the online version is what tracks your real percentages and monthly pace.
