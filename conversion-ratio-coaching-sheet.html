What Your Numbers Are Telling You
Compare each personal ratio to the standard above. A number worse than the standard usually points to a specific, fixable skill —
  • Contacts-to-lead worse than 12:1 — work on the qualifying conversation itself, not just dialing more.
  • Leads-to-appointment worse than 5:1 — sharpen the appointment-setting script and follow-up timing.
  • Appointments-to-closing worse than 2:1 — look at the presentation, objection handling, or the quality of appointments being set.
