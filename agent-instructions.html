Agent Instructions
How to use the sheet, week to week
The core idea: consistent daily action — not perfection — is what builds a lasting business. This sheet is here to make your numbers visible, not to punish you for an off week.

What this is

Your Weekly Goal Sheet is where you set your numbers for the week and track them daily. There are two versions:

  • Online Weekly Goal Sheet — use this whenever you have a laptop or desktop. It calculates your percentages automatically and remembers your goals week to week.
  • Paper Worksheet — use this when you're away from a computer (a meeting, a coaching session, on the go). Fill it out by hand, then transfer the numbers into the online sheet as soon as you're back at a computer.

Getting started

  1. Open the Weekly Goal Sheet and enter your name and the week you're setting goals for.
  2. Work through each section below, filling in your goals before the week begins.
  3. Log your actual activity daily — don't wait until Friday. The steppers (+ / −) make it quick to update as you go.

Lead Generation

The team standard is 9:30 AM – 12:30 PM, with a 2-hour minimum of focused lead generation every day.

  • If you can hit that window, log your start and end time for the day.
  • If a conflict comes up (an appointment, a doctor's visit, a client who's only free in the morning), that's fine — use "+ Add time block" to log a second block later in the day (e.g. 3:00–4:00 PM). Your total time for the day is what counts toward the 2-hour goal.
  • Each day shows a big status marker:
    Red X — not yet at 2 hours Flame + "Great Job!" — goal met
    It flashes when your status changes, then settles — that's your visual cue to check in.

Core Success Metrics

This section is what matters most — the numbers that actually predict a closed transaction, not just busywork.

Two-Way Conversations Goal (combined)

  • One shared goal fed by two activities: Two-Way Conversations (a real back-and-forth — a phone call, or even a conversation at Walmart where real estate comes up and the other person knows you're an agent) and Two-Way Text Conversations (an actual exchange — they replied, not just a text you sent).
  • Set your Monthly Goal once — after you enter it and click away, it locks in for the month.
  • Set your Weekly Goal — how much of that monthly number you're committing to this week. It also locks once entered.
  • If you set a weekly goal below the pace your monthly goal needs, you'll see a caution message with the exact make-up math for your remaining weeks. It's not a scolding — it's math helping you plan.
  • Log your actual conversations and texts as they happen, using the steppers.

Leads Generated, Appointments Set, Appointments Held, Listings Taken, Buyer Agreements Signed, Contracts Written/Offers Submitted, Closings

  • Each has its own Monthly Goal and Weekly Goal — same locking behavior (enter once, it locks).
  • These are separate from the conversations goal — they don't combine with each other.

Supporting Activities

Calls Made, Emails Sent, Database Touches, FB/IG Engagement, Open Houses, Referrals Given/Received, Reviews Requested/Received — still worth tracking (especially FB/IG for brand awareness), but not part of your weekly goal. Just log your counts here for your own visibility.

A few important things to know

  • Once a goal is locked, it can't be edited — the only way to change it is hitting "Start Fresh Week," which resets your counts for a new week (your goals carry over from last week so you don't have to retype them, but they unlock so you can adjust).
  • Your data is saved automatically in your browser, on your device — it doesn't sync to other devices. Use the same computer/browser each week so your history and monthly totals stay accurate.
  • If you started your week on the paper worksheet, transfer those numbers into the online sheet as soon as you can.
Questions? Talk to your team lead if anything about the goals, the lead-gen window, or the tool itself is unclear.
