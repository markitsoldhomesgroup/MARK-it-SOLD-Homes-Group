The Season: January 1 – November 15
This is the window your annual goals are built on — not a full 12 months.
Jan 1MarMayJulSepNov 15
Why November 15? The average transaction closes on a government loan (USDA, VA, or FHA), and those typically take about 45 days to close. A contract written after November 15 is unlikely to close before December 31. Every goal, pace check, and recalibration in this session is measured against this window — not the calendar year.

What Counts
Before we look at numbers, we agree on the definitions — every number on this page is built on these
A Contact
The starting point
  • ✓ A genuine two-way conversation, not a one-sided pitch
  • ✓ Real estate comes up in that conversation
  • ✓ The other person knows you're a real estate agent
A Lead
Qualified, not just interested
  • ✓ A stated timeframe to transact
  • ✓ Their name
  • ✓ Their phone number
  • ✓ Their mailing address
Timeframe must be within the current 12 months or the following 12 months — otherwise it's a contact to keep nurturing, not a lead yet.
